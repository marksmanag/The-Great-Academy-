# The Great Academy

> A constitutional knowledge operating system designed to transform curiosity into wisdom, and wisdom into disciplined action.

## Mission

The Great Academy is a knowledge architecture that transforms:

**Curiosity → Knowledge → Wisdom → Disciplined Action → Capability → Legacy**

Our focus is not on technology as a product. **Transformation is the product.**

## Foundational Principles

1. **Study the Past** — Learn from evidence, not speculation
2. **Understand the Present** — Build on what is known and proven
3. **Build the Future** — Create systems that outlive their creators
4. **Remain Teachable** — Adapt when evidence demands it
5. **Seek Truth Above Certainty** — Prefer evidence over convenience
6. **Transform Knowledge Into Wisdom** — Connect knowledge to disciplined action
7. **Transform Wisdom Into Disciplined Action** — Make decisions and live with their consequences
8. **Build Systems That Outlive Their Creators** — Design for sustainability and succession

## What This Repository Contains

This repository is the **canonical, version-controlled source of truth** for:

- **Constitution** — Foundational principles and values
- **Academy** — Learning frameworks, methodology, and governance
- **Architecture** — System design, knowledge structures, and AI methodology
- **Research** — Evidence, methodology, and decision records
- **Projects** — Implementations that apply Academy principles
- **Governance** — How we make decisions and maintain quality

## Repository Principles

### Clarity of Authority

This repository distinguishes carefully between:

- **CANONICAL** — Authoritative Great Academy constitutional material
- **WORKING** — Material currently being developed or evaluated
- **RESEARCH** — Evidence, references, experiments, and external findings
- **PROJECT** — Implementations that apply Academy principles
- **ARCHIVED** — Superseded or historical material retained for provenance

### No Fabrication

We do not:
- Invent missing doctrine
- Silently rewrite principles
- Fabricate facts
- Treat assumptions as evidence

We clearly distinguish:

- **EVIDENCE** — Observed facts, data, and tested findings
- **INFERENCE** — Logical conclusions from evidence
- **HYPOTHESIS** — Testable propositions not yet verified
- **DECISION** — Deliberate choices based on principles and evidence
- **UNKNOWN** — What we don't know and need to learn
