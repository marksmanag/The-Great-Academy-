# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Great Academy is committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for all participants. We believe that diverse perspectives, backgrounds, and experiences strengthen our work.

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free community experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Assuming good intent in discussions and disagreements
- Engaging with evidence and reasoning
- Acknowledging mistakes and learning from them
- Respecting the intellectual contributions of others
- Crediting sources and prior work

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate
- Spreading misinformation or fabricated claims
- Dismissing evidence without reasoning
- Refusing to engage with good-faith disagreement

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders (CODEOWNERS and maintainers) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
- Explain decisions transparently

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the CODEOWNERS through:

1. **GitHub Issues** — For concerns that can be public
2. **Direct communication** — For sensitive concerns requiring privacy
3. **Email** — Private report (to be established)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

**Community leaders are obligated to:**

- Maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter
- Not retaliate against anyone for reporting in good faith
- Respond within a reasonable timeframe
- Explain the decision and any actions taken

## Appeals

If you believe a decision was made in error, you may appeal through the same channels that reported the concern.

## Consequences

Inappropriate behavior will result in consequences determined by community leaders, which may include:

1. **Reminder** — First violation may result in a private conversation and reminder of standards
2. **Temporary restrictions** — Repeated violations may result in temporary restrictions on participation
3. **Permanent ban** — Serious or persistent violations may result in removal from the community

## Our Principles

This Code of Conduct is grounded in The Great Academy's foundational principles:

- **Seek truth above certainty** — We engage with evidence and reasoning
- **Remain teachable** — We listen to correction and adapt
- **Transform knowledge into wisdom** — We consider impact and consequence
- **Build systems that outlive their creators** — We create inclusive, sustainable communities
